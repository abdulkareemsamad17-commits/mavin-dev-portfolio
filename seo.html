SEO

Build a clearer search foundation so people and search engines can better understand your store and its products. We’ll look at how your pages are organized, how product information is presented, and which practical updates fit your platform and priorities.

What's included

  • Technical foundations: Review page indexing signals, site structure, and common technical issues on agreed pages.
  • On-page elements: Check titles, descriptions, headings, and internal links for clarity and consistency.
  • Product and collection pages: Identify where page copy or organization could better explain what you sell.
  • Prioritized recommendations: Separate practical near-term fixes from larger opportunities for later.
  • Next-step guide: Leave you with an action list for maintaining and building on the improvements.

How it works

  1. Audit: Review agreed store pages, navigation, page-level SEO elements, and any available search data to understand the starting point.
  2. Plan: Turn the review into a prioritized scope, confirming which changes are included and what requires separate work.
  3. Implement: Make the agreed updates to page content, links, or settings, and flag anything that depends on your platform or other providers.
  4. Measure and report: Summarize completed changes and review available search signals as a baseline; search visibility changes are not guaranteed.
