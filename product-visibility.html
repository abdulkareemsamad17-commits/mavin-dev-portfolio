Product visibility

Present and organize product information clearly so shoppers can discover and understand what your store offers. We’ll look at the quality and consistency of your catalog, how people browse it, and any relevant product channels you already use.

What's included

  • Catalog review: Check agreed products for completeness and consistency across titles, descriptions, variants, and attributes.
  • Product organization: Review categories, collections, filters, and the paths shoppers use to find relevant items.
  • Product content: Identify opportunities to make product details easier to scan and understand.
  • Channel readiness: Review the product data used by relevant discovery channels, where those channels and account access are available.
  • Improvement plan: Define catalog updates and any repeatable standards for adding or maintaining products.

How it works

  1. Audit: Review a representative selection of your catalog, browse structure, and relevant channel requirements.
  2. Plan: Agree which products, fields, and discovery paths to address first, based on your goals and catalog size.
  3. Implement: Update the agreed product information or store organization and document any catalog-wide guidance.
  4. Measure and report: Check that agreed information is present and review available channel diagnostics; placement and visibility are not guaranteed.
