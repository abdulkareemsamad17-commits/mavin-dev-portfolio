PLATFORM MIGRATION

Moving to a new platform? I’ll help you do it safely.

Moving platforms can affect rankings and sales if handled carelessly, so I plan the work step by step and check what can be carried across before we begin.

What I migrate

  • Products and collections.
  • Customers, where the platforms allow it.
  • Orders, where the platforms allow it.
  • Content and pages.
  • URLs and their redirect destinations.

How it works

  1. Review the current store

    We look at the store and agree what needs to move.

  2. Plan the structure and redirects

    I map the new structure and plan redirects from old URLs.

  3. Move and rebuild

    I move agreed data and rebuild the pages in the destination platform.

  4. Test key journeys

    I check links, checkout, emails and tracking with you before launch.

  5. Launch and monitor

    After launch, I check rankings, traffic and errors using available data.

Protect your SEO during the move

  • Plan 301 redirects from old URLs to the closest relevant new pages.
  • Preserve existing page titles and meta descriptions where appropriate.
  • Check Search Console after launch for indexing and crawl errors.

Redirects and checks can reduce migration risk, but they cannot guarantee rankings will stay unchanged.

Platforms I migrate between

  • Wix to Shopify.
  • Shopify to Wix.
  • WordPress to Shopify.

Pricing and timeline

Pricing and timeline are discussed based on what your store needs: the platforms involved, the amount of work and any custom requirements. After the free audit, I’ll send a clear quote and a realistic estimate before any work begins.

For a migration, the estimate also depends on the size of the store, the amount of data and any custom work.

An honest note about store data

Some data, such as order history or reviews, can’t always be moved perfectly. I’ll check what each platform supports and explain what is possible before we start.
