WooCommerce growth

I review your WooCommerce store and agree practical improvements for search, performance, checkout and customer follow-up.

  1. What I improve on WooCommerce

    • Store structure and product-page foundations with SEO.
    • Product, cart and checkout journeys with conversion optimization.
    • Customer signup and follow-up with email retention.

WooCommerce questions

Why is my store slow?

Speed can be affected by hosting, caching, plugins, themes, images and other factors. I can review the setup to understand what may be contributing before suggesting changes.

Can you work on a live store safely?

I use backups and, where available, a staging copy to review and test agreed changes before they go live. We’ll confirm the approach for your setup before I begin.

Do I need to change plugins?

Not automatically. I can review what each plugin does, check for overlap or conflicts, and discuss options with you before anything is changed.
