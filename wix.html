Wix store growth

I review your Wix store and agree practical changes for search, mobile shopping, customer follow-up and product visibility, within Wix’s capabilities.

  1. What I improve on Wix

    • Page structure and search settings with SEO.
    • Mobile layout and shopping steps with conversion optimization.
    • Customer signup and follow-up with email retention.
    • Product details and channel listings with product visibility.
    • Store content and settings where Wix provides a suitable option.

  2. Things I check

    • Page speed, heavy design elements and image weight.

These are things I check, not findings about any store.

Wix questions

Can a Wix store rank well?

Wix includes SEO settings and tools. Search visibility depends on many factors, including your content, competition and technical setup; I can review what is available for your store.

Should I move away from Wix?

Not necessarily. I can first review your goals and what Wix supports, then discuss whether your current setup can meet your needs or whether a move is worth considering.

What can’t you change on Wix?

Some underlying platform behavior and system features are controlled by Wix. I’ll explain which requested changes the platform allows before I include them in a scope.
