Platform questions

Which platform should I use?

It depends on your products, team, store needs and the tools you rely on. I can review your priorities with you before recommending a direction.

Do you work with my platform if it’s not listed?

Tell me which platform you use and what you need to change. I’ll check what it supports and be clear about what I can help with.

Can you improve my store without changing platform?

Yes. We can focus on changes within your current platform where they fit your goals and what the platform allows.

Can you help me sell on marketplaces like Amazon or Etsy?

Yes. I help with setup and optimization for the marketplace you use, including listings, product data, images and visibility.

Can you move my store to another platform?

Yes. See how I approach platform migration.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

Pricing and timeline are discussed based on what your store needs: the platforms involved, the amount of work and any custom requirements. After the free audit, I’ll send a clear quote and a realistic estimate before any work begins.
